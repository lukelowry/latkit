Explore the diagram

  • Make it yours

    Move blocks, draw wires, reconnect an input. Undo and redo every edit.

  • Follow a signal

    Enable flow, arrows, and live values. Color maps and shades respond to native fields.

  • Go beyond pairs

    The control loop branches one signal to two destinations. A wire can join many ports.

  • Fold a hierarchy

    Open Grouped plants. Collapse a unit or its parent station; external wires follow its boundary.

  • Change the geometry

    Try four shapes, three routers, tags, layered layout, and a custom grid strategy.

  • Work at scale

    The 1,024-block scene uses the same API. Zoom in to reveal labels and inspect resident geometry.

  • Keep the result

    Export a fixed-time PNG through an independent offscreen renderer.

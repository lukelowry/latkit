# Track power flows during a fault

```{image} ../images/fault.png
:alt: The ACTIVSg25k grid as a dim web, after a fault at the bus marked in red near New York. Corridors of branches spreading southwest from it glow amber and white where their flow changed most
:class: picture
```

The image shows ACTIVSg25k following a fault at bus 69645. The fault clears at 1.15 s.
Brightness shows how much each branch's real power changed. The comets show the direction of that change.

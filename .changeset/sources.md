'latkit': minor
---

Views draw sources, and say so in one word each: a `Data` its latest version, a `Snapshot` as it is, `Tiles` fetched in pieces. `load()` loads the WebAssembly and every class imports from `latkit`; every wait ends at an abort signal; colors and lengths say what they are; a table too large to draw whole draws pages of its order that keep their place as the camera moves, and edges ending far apart draw within the budget.

- Added: `view.sources` and `gpu.view(scene, { sources })`: `Data`, drawn at its latest version from the frame after each patch applies; `Snapshot`, a version kept as it is (`data.snapshot()`, `data.restore(snapshot)`); and `Tiles(name, url)`, time tiles or vector tiles, fetched and shaped once for every view of a GPU. `view.snapshot(name)` is what a view draws of a source now.
- Added: `load({ wasm, worker })`; a call before it finishes throws `unloaded`. Every class and builder imports from `latkit`.
- Added: every wait takes `{ signal }` and ends at it, 30 seconds unless given: picks, `screenOf`, `arrange`, pictures, encodings, videos, `gpu.done()`, `gpu.landed()`. A timeout rejects with `timeout`, an abort or a destroyed view with `cancelled`, and the work under way is dropped.
- Added: `gpu.canvas(element, { layout: [{ view, at }] })` and `canvas.layout`; `canvas.shot()`, a picture of exactly what a canvas shows; `new Shot(size, { scale, layout, transparent, capture, elapsed })`, where `capture: 'now'` takes views as they stand; `shot.video({ fps, output, bitrate, signal })`.
- Added: colors are typed: in a color option a string is a CSS color, `field(name)` reads a field, and `color(css)`, colormaps, and `when(...)` make colors (`Paint`, `PaintInput`).
- Added: `px(n)` and `units(n)` say a length's unit: CSS pixels on a plane, the earth, and a plot, position units in a diagram, unless said. A stroke in pixels keeps its width as a diagram zooms.
- Added: the theme in groups: `text`, `vertex`, `edge`, `line`, `area`, `focus`, `flow`, `diagram`, `earth`, `plot`; a comet's `flow.spacing` and `flow.length` take `{ px }` or `{ units }`. A diagram's `diagram.outline`, `corner`, `portSize`, and `bend` set its blocks' and groups' borders, their corners, its ports' width, and its wires' bends; `shadow: true` on its blocks and groups lays a soft shadow `diagram.shadow.lift` below them.
- Added: `Gpu.resident` and `Gpu.uploading`, bytes of data on the GPU and waiting to upload for every view of it; `Work.packed`, `Work.resolved` (rows whose ends were found where their vertices are held), and `Work.cleared` (bytes of pages cleared).
- Changed: `view.scene`, `view.selection`, `view.screen(position)`, `view.position(point)`, and `view.screenOf(row)`; the `hovered` and `selection` events; `view.waiting`.
- Changed: `new Patch(dataset)` names a patch's dataset; `Patch.read(source)` reads a patch's bytes or JSON; `Data.read(source, { name, base })` a dataset's bytes, JSON, or GeoJSON; `patch.append(table, at, fields, { ids })` and `patch.frames(table, field, { level, first, at, values })`; `data.envelope({ table, field, range, spans, ids })`; `field(name).from(data, table)` reads another dataset.
- Changed: the `'earth'` space, `{ earth: { projection: 'mercator' | 'globe', graticule, daylight, atmosphere } }`; the ground's color is the theme's `earth.ground`.
- Changed: edges ending far apart draw within the budget: the vertices they end at are held a row at a time where few lie in a page, and edges find where each end is held on the GPU whenever the vertices move.
- Changed: a plot measures the room its axes' labels and titles take.
- Changed: a table drawn in part holds whole pages of its order, each in a place of its own, and a page it no longer draws stays held while the plan leaves room, so drawing it again costs nothing; appending to it costs a page and never thins it; lanes, the stars of nets, joins, and fitted domains read the data, not the rows drawn.
- Changed: a view's first frame waits only for what draws its marks, a browser's first GPU is made while the WebAssembly loads, pipelines compile through one queue per GPU, the most urgent first, and only for what their marks are and read; the wasm is 12% smaller.
- Changed: a hit names the row and the place the frame it answers drew; a GPU's views draw one frame together, `Budget.uploadPerFrame` bounding what all of them upload in it.
- Changed: a diagram's blocks and groups are bordered in `diagram.outline`; corners, status rings, and ports' rings zoom with the drawing; a block makes room for its top and bottom ports' labels; text is set in the system's sans-serif face unless the theme names one.
- Changed: the globe's atmosphere is a thin rim and a halo; a GPU decides its antialiasing once; zooming at a fit's limit keeps the center.
- Removed: `view.setData`, `view.setTiles`, `view.removeData`, and `view.data(name)` (`view.sources`); `view.show(scene)`, `view.select(rows)`, `view.point`, `view.ground`, and `view.locate`; the `hover` and `select` events; `Stats.waiting`, `Stats.uploading`, `Stats.resident`, and `Stats.budget`.
- Removed: `loadLatkit()` and its result object, and `latkit/core`; `createWorker` (`worker`); `{ deadline }` options; `canvas.show(views)` and the canvas's `panels`; `Shot`'s builder methods, `shot.image(views)`, and `shot.video(gpu, fps, output)`.
- Removed: `Patch.to`, `Patch.fromBytes`, `Data.load`, `Data.tables`, `data.ids()`, `data.fields()`, `data.span` (`describe()`), and the positional `envelope`; `expr.of(data, table)` (`from`).
- Removed: the `'mercator'` and `'globe'` spaces; the theme's flat keys, such as `vertexRadius`, `fontSize`, and `edgeSpacing`; a string read as a color only when it began with `#`; `Flow.units`; shadows on edges, lines, areas, and faces, and the theme's `shadow.light` and `shadow.softness`.
- Fixed: a view whose next version finds no room lets go of what it draws so that version lands; where even that fits nothing, it tells a `limit` error and rests. A mark ordered by `priority` draws the whole trace it traces. A scene with two marks of one kind drawing one table asks for a `name`. A plot's axes start below its title.
